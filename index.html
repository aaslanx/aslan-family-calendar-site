aAslan Family Calendar

The family's week, on the wall

A shared calendar and chore board for a tablet that stays on all day · Android

What it does. Everyone's events in their own colour, chores that come back on their own, and an optional link to the Google calendars you already use. Everything is stored on the tablet itself — no accounts, no servers, no ads.

Features

  • Timeline (3 / 5 / 7 days), agenda, week and month views
  • One colour per household member; drag an event to move it
  • Chores with steps, priority and repeat rules, grouped by person or by day
  • Optional Google Calendar sync — pick calendars, one-way or two-way
  • "Mark as important": the day gets a red ring so nobody misses it
  • Kiosk mode for a wall-mounted tablet; works fully offline
